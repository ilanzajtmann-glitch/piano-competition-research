# Van Cliburn 2025 Audit

## 1. What was collected
- 28 official competitors with nationality/representation text and profile source URLs.
- Verified advancement flags: preliminary, quarterfinalist, semifinalist, finalist.
- Medal ranks and named special prizes from official Cliburn sources.
- 82 performance/program occurrences by candidate and round.
- 24 work rows, including all final-round concerto entries from the official schedule and Carter Johnson candidate-page repertoire captured from official text.

## 6. Completeness percentage
- Candidate/results completeness: 100% for expected count, candidate source URLs, result source URLs, and reached-round flags.
- Performance occurrence completeness: 100% for sourced round-level performance occurrence rows.
- Work-level repertoire completeness: not complete; currently a conservative partial capture. Treat this as an auditable seed dataset, not a finished repertoire corpus.

## 7. Known missing data
- Birth years are null because exact birth dates were not captured from official sources.
- Full work-level solo repertoire for 27 candidates remains to be captured from official candidate pages or official performance pages.
- Composer metadata such as birth/death years, nationality, and periods remains blank pending controlled authority work.
- Some feature values are necessarily conservative where work rows are not yet populated.

## 8. Validation results
- Candidate count check: passed.
- Result consistency check: passed.
- Source URL check: passed.
- Duplicate work check: passed.
- Missing data report: passed as a report, with known nulls documented.

## 9. Whether the dataset is ready for statistical analysis
No. The dataset is ready for source-review and continued work-level data collection, but it is not ready for statistical analysis because full repertoire has not yet been collected and validated.
